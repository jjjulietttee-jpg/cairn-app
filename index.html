Field notes for your pocket

Specimen sheet / revised 6 Oct 2026

Cairn is an iPhone app for people who pick things up off the ground and want to know what they are holding. Photograph a stone, read the shortlist of likely minerals and rocks, check the reasoning against the handbook, then file the specimen in your own cabinet with a place on the map.

Identifies
Rocks and minerals, from a photo you take or choose
Keeps
A personal cabinet of specimens, notes and found-here pins, stored on your phone
Needs an account?
No. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into
Paid part
Cairn Pro, a subscription that unlocks reading and saving results

Privacy lists exactly which bits leave your phone and who gets them. Terms covers the subscription and the limits of a photo-based guess. Help answers the questions we actually get.

NOTEOne honest line

A guess from a photograph is a guess. For anything you plan to sell, eat from, grind, or handle in bulk, ask a lab or a local geology society.
