Help desk

Questions we actually get / 6 October 2026

Write to nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru and mention your iPhone model and iOS version. Replies usually come within two working days.

I paid but Pro is locked.

Open Preferences and tap Restore purchases while using the same Apple ID that made the purchase. If the sheet says nothing is found, mail us the date and the Apple receipt number.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Cairn, Cancel. Do it before renewal day to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel.

The plans did not load.

Check the connection and tap Try again. If the App Store has not answered yet, Cairn tells you so. A VPN or a cellular restriction on the App Store is the usual cause.

How do I delete a specimen or all my data?

Open the specimen, choose Remove from cabinet, confirm. To clear everything, delete the app. Details are in Privacy.

Cairn named the wrong rock.

Try a dry, clean surface, daylight, and a second photo of a fresh break or streak. Compare the shortlist with the Handbook entries, since the app shows several candidates rather than one verdict.

Why does it want my location?

Only when you tap Here on an entry, to pin where you found it. You can leave location off in iOS Settings under Cairn.

Is it usable without a signal?

The cabinet, handbook and map pins you already saved open offline. Identifying from a photo needs a connection.

What does Reset first run do?

It replays the intro screens. It does not touch your specimens or subscription.
