What leaves your phone

Privacy notice / 6 October 2026

Short version: your cabinet lives on your iPhone. A photo leaves it only at the moment you ask Cairn to identify something. Everything else below is the detail.

ItemWhere it goesWhy

01The photo trip

When you tap identify, the picture is shrunk to a JPEG and posted over HTTPS to a small function we run on Google Cloud. The function forwards it to OpenAI's API with an instruction to name likely minerals and rocks and what was visible. The answer comes back to your phone. Our function does not write the photo to storage or a database; its logs record only error codes. OpenAI handles the image under its own API terms while it works on it.

02Camera, photo library, location

The camera is used when you shoot a specimen. The photo library is read only for pictures you pick one at a time. Location is requested while the app is open, and only when you press Here on an entry; Cairn never tracks you in the background and never reads your location otherwise. Say no to any of them and the rest still works.

03Adapty and the subscription

Cairn uses Adapty to manage Pro. Adapty receives an anonymous profile ID, your device identifier, the App Store purchase record, and counts of paywall views and button taps. It does not receive your photos, notes, or pins. We do not use advertising SDKs and do not track you across other apps.

04If you write to us

The “not happy?” prompt opens your mail app with the app version, iOS version and your Adapty profile ID filled in so we can find your purchase. You can delete those lines before sending.

05Getting rid of things

Open a specimen and choose Remove from cabinet, then confirm with Remove; the entry, its photo and its pin are erased from the phone. To wipe everything at once, delete Cairn from your home screen. There is no copy on our side to ask us for, because we never receive your cabinet. For the Adapty profile or any other data question, write to nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru.

06Children

Cairn is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly gather information from children under 13.

07Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, the date at the top moves and the app's version notes say so.
